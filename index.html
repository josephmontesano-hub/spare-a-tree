Frequently Asked Questions

What happens to the tree I sponsor?+

Your sponsored tree is immediately flagged in our system as protected, Saran wrapped to the top, and removed from the active clearing queue for the duration of its Active Sponsorship Window.

How is my dollar used?+

Your contribution supports Snr. Buns ongoing infrastructure expansion into data mining and machine learning. Including but not limited to data center hardware, land development, and administrative processing associated with the Spare-A-Tree Program.

What are the full Program Terms?+

We believe in full transparency. Here is the complete legal text TLDR of the Spare-A-Tree Program:

By participating in the Spare-A-Tree Program ("Program"), you acknowledge and agree that your sponsored tree ("Sponsored Growth Asset") is protected under Snr. Buns Adaptive Canopy Prioritization Policy for the duration of its Active Sponsorship Window. The Active Sponsorship Window begins immediately upon receipt of payment and concludes automatically upon enrollment of the next Sponsored Growth Asset contributor by a subsequent Program participant, at which time clearing operations may resume in the original service area as necessary to meet Snr. Buns data center expansion timelines. Sponsorship is non-transferable, non-refundable, and does not guarantee permanent preservation of any specific tree, root system, or canopy area. Snr. Buns Corporate thanks you for your commitment to a smarter, greener tomorrow.
